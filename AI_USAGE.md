# AI-assisted development

AI-assisted development tools may be used while implementing and reviewing Maglucen Stardew Valley Companion.

The maintainer remains responsible for product direction, testing, licensing decisions, release approval, and the behavior of published binaries. Generated suggestions are treated as untrusted until they are reviewed against the source, local game data, automated checks, and observed application behavior.

The project does not use generative AI inside the installed application, upload save data to an AI service, or require an AI account at runtime.

External platforms may require an AI-related label for a listing or release. The project will follow each platform's current disclosure and categorization rules.
